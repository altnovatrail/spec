# The documents of spec

What spec holds, how its releases come out and are verified, and how to contribute to it: what the [README](../README.md) of the repository leaves out, which says only what spec is and where it stands among NovaTrail's repositories. The repositories themselves, their roles and the order they appear in are in [repositories.md](repositories.md).

## Documents

- [product.md](product.md) — what we build and why: who it is for, the principles, the first slice, the roadmap and the open product questions.
- [concepts.md](concepts.md) — the shared vocabulary: module, skill, lesson, task, canvas, check, tutor, learner model and engine, with a glossary that names each term as code and schemas will.
- [privacy.md](privacy.md) — privacy and age: kinds of data, age categories, the two routes to AI, and what each AI provider's terms say about minors.
- [architecture.md](architecture.md) — the platform's big parts and how they connect: the components, one overall diagram, the main flows, and how the interface is put together.
- [repositories.md](repositories.md) — how the platform is split into repositories: their roles, dependencies, how changes flow between them, the order they appear in, how to set one up, and what is carried over from MathTrail.
- [dpia.md](dpia.md) — the data protection impact assessment: the processing, its necessity, the risks to children and the measures against them, and what must hold before the first child.
- [decisions.md](decisions.md) — the decision log: what was decided, why, and what was rejected.
- [sources.md](sources.md) — the sources the documents rest on: providers' terms, laws, standards, other programs' documentation and MathTrail's texts, each with its date and its quote.
- [measurements.md](measurements.md) — the results of measurements: where the tokens of the Claude Code sessions go, and what each change gave.
- [admission/](../admission/README.md) — the admission table: to which AI provider, on which route to AI, the work of a learner of each age category may go, and which capability of AI may serve each category, with the facts of a deployment that open a cell. Its text, the table, its schemas, examples and test vectors, and `just admission-check` checks them.
- [deployment/](../deployment/README.md) — the deployment configuration: the file `/deployment.json` that the learner's app reads at its start, with the origin of the platform's API and the parameters of the sign-in service, and the rules by which a reader accepts it. Its text, its schemas, examples and test vectors, and `just deployment-check` checks them.
- [legal/](../legal/README.md) — the legal texts of the platform: the privacy policy, the terms of use, the consents, and the sets of texts of the samples page with the volunteer's form, each in versions numbered from 1 that never change once in force, with their gist in the languages of the interface. The app gets them from the API, and the samples page and the organizer from a release file. Its text, the passport, the texts, their schemas, examples and test vectors, and `just legal-check` checks them.
- [consent-age/](../consent-age/README.md) — the table of the age of digital consent: for each country of the EU and the EEA, the United Kingdom and the United States, the age from which a learner gives consent themselves, from the day it applies, with the law and its provision, a quote, the source and the date it was read; a country outside the table has 18. The API puts a learner in an age category by it. Its text, the table, their schemas, examples and test vectors, and `just consent-age-check` checks them.
- [export/](../export/README.md) — the format of the export of a learner's profile or of a whole account: a ZIP of files of JSON, with the part of the API byte for byte, what the device holds, and the evidence log and the model's state as the JSON of their protobuf messages beside their `.proto` files. The app builds it, and anyone reads it with no library. Its text, its schemas, examples and test vectors, and `just export-check` checks them.
- [contracts/](../contracts/README.md) — the API contracts: protobuf files by buf's rules, and the code buf generates from them for Go and TypeScript, with the observation and the eval protocol's sample file among them. `just contracts-check` checks them.
- [format/](../format/README.md) — the module format: what a module may and must contain and how the platform checks it. Its text, the schemas of the manifest, the skill graph, the trap and technique catalogs, the solving steps, the task, with the checks of the task's answer slots, its hints and its templates, and the lesson, the generator of randomness, the shared taxonomy of skills, the layout of a module, the package of a module — its canonical view and its inventory — and the module index, which names every published version of every module, are written, with their examples, the test vectors of the YAML rules, of a whole module, of the manifest, of the skill graph, of the trap catalog, of the task and of the lesson, and the test vectors of the open view, of verdicts, of the leak check, of the generator, of templates, of tiers, of packages and of the module index, and `just format-check` checks them; the other parts of a module come as they are written.
- [RULES.md](../RULES.md) — the rules every NovaTrail repository shares, and [repo-template/](../repo-template/README.md), the template every other repository starts from.
- [RUN.md](../RUN.md) — the work plan, in epics: the current epic broken into small tasks, run and reviewed one at a time.
- `run/` — the plan's other tables: [the epics that come later](../run/EPICS.md), [the open questions to the author](../run/QUESTIONS.md), [the technical debt](../run/DEBT.md) — what was put off on purpose, what it risks and when to return — [what spec has recorded of the other repositories' rows](../run/INBOX.md) and [the open tasks outside an epic](../run/TASKS.md).
- [COORDINATION.md](../COORDINATION.md) — the cross-repository plan, its order in Russian: in what order the other repositories' tasks run, by the order in which the repositories appear, and which task waits for which, each row pointing to a task in that repository's RUN.md.
- [draft/](../draft/README.md) — a clickable prototype of the learner's app, and where it differs from the documents.

## Releases

A release comes out by itself once the checks pass on `main`: the next patch of the current line, tagged `vX.Y.Z` with the release, and nobody tags by hand. The module format has a version of its own, and a change that gives it a new version always starts a new line; so do the admission table, the deployment configuration, the legal texts, the table of the age of digital consent and the format of the export. Other repositories take its tag as the `spec` submodule, for the documents and rules, and their code takes the release's files:

- each part as an archive: `format-<version>.tar.gz` and `format-<version>.tar.gz.sigstore.json`, and so `admission-`, `deployment-`, `legal-`, `consent-age-` and `export-<version>.tar.gz`, the version being the part's `VERSION`;
- Go code, the same tag as the version of the Go module `github.com/altnovatrail/spec`, whose package holds the parts as `spec.Format`, `spec.Admission`, `spec.Deployment`, `spec.Legal`, `spec.ConsentAge` and `spec.Export`, with their types in the packages of `types/`, one a part and its major version, such as `types/formatv0`, `types/formatv0/open` and `types/consentagev1`;
- TypeScript, the same types as the npm package `@altnovatrail/spec` in `altnovatrail-spec-<version>.tgz`, by the same names, such as `@altnovatrail/spec/admissionv0`, with the types of the element `<novatrail-canvas>` as `@altnovatrail/spec/canvas`: the contract between `canvas` and `app`, written by hand in `types/canvas/`. The package in npm is that file, which the release publishes there itself, by trusted publishing;
- the code of the API contracts, generated from [contracts/](../contracts/README.md): Go code takes it from the Go module `github.com/altnovatrail/spec/contracts`, at the tag `contracts/vX.Y.Z` that the release sets on the same commit, and TypeScript from the npm package `@altnovatrail/contracts` in `altnovatrail-contracts-<version>.tgz`, which [its README](../tools/contracts/npm/README.md) describes, published the same way.

Each file of a release is signed with cosign, its bundle `<file>.sigstore.json` beside it, as `format-<version>.tar.gz.sigstore.json`.

A repository moves the `spec` submodule, both Go modules and both npm packages together, at one tag, and leaves them out of Dependabot: a release comes out with each change of a document. Releases are immutable, so a tag stays on its commit and no file changes once it is out, and GitHub attests each one with its files. A file of a release, spec's or any NovaTrail repository's, is checked by `just release-verify`, with its bundle beside it, and the packages of `@altnovatrail` in an npm lock file by `just release-verify-npm`. They check the file's signature against Sigstore's trust root and everything its certificate names — who signed, at whose call, on which branch and of which commit — and, for a file of spec, the commit or version the file itself holds; that is the check NovaTrail's code and CI rely on. Another repository runs them from its submodule, `just -f spec/justfile release-verify …`. The attestation, which ties a file to a release rather than to a commit, `gh release verify` and `gh release verify-asset` check apart:

```
just release-verify spec v0.5.6 format-0.1.tar.gz
just release-verify spec v0.7.0 altnovatrail-contracts-0.7.0.tgz
just release-verify-npm package-lock.json
gh release verify v0.5.6 -R altnovatrail/spec
gh release verify-asset v0.5.6 format-0.1.tar.gz -R altnovatrail/spec
```

## Contributing

Report a bug or propose a change in an [issue](https://github.com/altnovatrail/spec/issues), in English, and open one before a pull request unless the pull request fixes a typo or a broken link. How a change comes in, with every commit signed off, and what every change keeps to are in the organization's [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/altnovatrail/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md); the rules every NovaTrail repository shares are in [RULES.md](../RULES.md). Report a vulnerability privately, as the [security policy](https://github.com/altnovatrail/spec/security/policy) says, never in a public issue.

Work in the devcontainer: open the repository in VS Code and choose "Reopen in Container". It holds every tool at the version the repository pins. The checks are recipes of the [justfile](../justfile), and `just --list` names each with its arguments:

- `just docs-check` checks the documents: links, cited sections, the registers and every number cited from them, Mermaid diagrams, the rules of `.claude/rules/` and the budgets of size of `tools/docs-check/budgets.json`;
- `just format-check` checks the module format in [format/](../format/README.md): its schemas, examples and test vectors, and computes the verdicts and traps of the vectors of verdicts, what the leak check finds in the vectors of the leak check and in the lessons of a module, the numbers of the vectors of the generator, the instances of the vectors of templates, with the results of their solvers as the vectors give them, the tiers of the vectors of tiers, the package of each module of the vectors of packages — its canonical view, the listing of its device part, its inventory and their digests —, and the module index that each vector of the index builds of its release, or the rule of the index that refuses it;
- `just admission-check` checks the admission table in [admission/](../admission/README.md): its schemas, the table and the rows of the providers' terms on age in [privacy.md](privacy.md) and the decisions it cites, its examples, and its test vectors, whose answers it computes as the admission function does;
- `just deployment-check` checks the format of the deployment configuration in [deployment/](../deployment/README.md): its schemas, examples and test vectors, whose files it reads as the app and the rollout read them, by the schema and then by the rules of reading;
- `just legal-check` checks the legal texts in [legal/](../legal/README.md): their schemas, examples and test vectors, and the part itself, read by the schema and then by its rules, against the part of the release before, so that a version in force never changes; a self-hosted copy checks its own texts by `just -f spec/justfile legal-check-dir <folder>`;
- `just consent-age-check` checks the table of the age of digital consent in [consent-age/](../consent-age/README.md): its schemas, examples and test vectors — of reading the table and of the category it gives a learner —, and the table itself, read by the schema and then by its rules, against the table of the release before, so that an age once given never changes;
- `just export-check` checks the format of the export in [export/](../export/README.md): its schemas, examples and test vectors of reading an export, unpacked, by the schemas and then by its rules;
- `just types-check` checks the types of the module format, of the admission table, of the deployment configuration, of the legal texts, of the table of the age of digital consent and of the files of an export under `types/`, in Go and TypeScript. They must be what `just types` generates from the schemas. Each value of `format/`, `admission/`, `deployment/`, `legal/`, `consent-age/` and `export/` that passes a schema must be read by its Go type and written back as it was, and `tsc` must take it as a value of its TypeScript type from the npm package. `tsc` must also take `tools/types/canvas.check.ts`, the check of the hand-written types of `<novatrail-canvas>` in `types/canvas/`, with the DOM and with the npm package of the contracts' code, whose message `Mark` the element's marks are held to;
- `just contracts-check` checks the API contracts in [contracts/](../contracts/README.md): buf's format, lint and comparison with the previous release, the rules of the contracts that buf does not hold, the code buf generates, which `just contracts` writes, and its npm package, which `tsc` and Node take, and computes the test vectors of the observation — whether each observation is valid, how two join and which verdicts the joined one gives —, which the module of the contracts carries as `contracts.Vectors`, and the test vectors of the rules the device and the runner share — how a history of writing folds, the groups of strokes, the crop, snapping and the boxes of a workbook —, which the module carries there too and the npm package by their paths, and holds the examples of the sample file of the eval protocol to their rules — the fields a sample's purpose and writer give it, its strokes, crops and their PNG, its markup, and the numbers of its crops and the groups and boxes of its history by the rules of the strokes — and the examples of the runner's report to its form and to the samples they name;
- `just go-check` checks the Go modules code takes from a release — the module at the root and the module of the contracts: what each requires, and its zip as the Go module proxy will build it;
- `just ci-lint`, `just ci-test`, `just ci-vuln` and `just ci-licenses` check every Go module of spec — the Go module and the modules of `tools/` — as the shared Go checks of every NovaTrail repository run them: `gofmt` and `go vet`, the tests with the race detector, govulncheck and go-licenses. `ci-test` writes the coverage of the tests to `coverage/go.out`;
- `just licenses-check` checks the licenses of the packages of `tools/`;
- `just release-version` prints the version the next release takes, and `just release-files HEAD <folder> <tag>` builds the files it carries, the npm packages of that tag among them;
- `just coverage` runs every test of `tools/` and writes their coverage to `coverage/lcov.info`, then the tests of the Go modules, as `ci-test` runs them.

A check prints a line for each problem it finds, `file:line: message` for the documents, the format and the contracts, and exits with a non-zero status if it finds any. CI runs them all on every pull request and on `main`, and a pull request merges only when they pass. Another NovaTrail repository checks its own documents with `just -f spec/justfile docs-check-repo`, and its sample files of the eval protocol with `just -f spec/justfile sample-check <file>…`, by the code that checks the examples. The environment and the checks are described in more detail in the Environment section of [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md).
