# NovaTrail — sources

> The external facts that the documents of `docs/` rest on: the terms of providers, laws, standards, the documentation of other programs and the texts of MathTrail. Here lie their records — the address, the version or the date of the page, the date read and the quote, — and the documents that describe how things are built and what the rules are hold the claim and a reference to the record: `(sources 4.1)`, and from another repository — `sources 4.1 spec` (RULES.md, The bar; R143).
>
> The records are grouped by topic. Until T133 they stood in architecture 8, privacy 10 and product 14; pointers here remain there, because the decision log and the archives of `docs/plan/` refer to those sections, and those are not rewritten (RUN.md, T133). The results of measurements are in [measurements.md](measurements.md).

## 1. How to keep it

- **A record** is a subsection "N.M" of a topic: one source, or one reading of several sources for the sake of one decision. It holds the address, the version or the date of the page, the date read, and the quote, in the language of the source. What was not read from the primary source — a copy, a pointer, a page that did not open — the record names as such.
- **A reference.** A claim of a document refers to a record by its number, as to a section of any document: `(sources 5.4)`. The number of a record does not change: a new record goes at the end of its topic, a new topic at the end of the file.
- **The source was read again** — an item with its own date is added to the record, and the earlier text is not edited: the decisions of its day rest on it. If the source changed and the claim diverged from it, that is an amendment to a decision or an open question, not an edit of the record.
- **What is not here:** the quotes that an entry of the decision log gives itself — they stay in the entry (RULES.md, How we work: a decision is never rewritten); the sources of the DPIA — they are in dpia 9, so that the assessment stays whole (R129); the source data of a part — the table of the age of digital consent has its own references and quotes, by country ([the table of the part `consent-age/`](../consent-age/consent-age.yaml)); the quotes on which the method of the measurements of E10 stands — the API prices in measurements 1.2 and the documentation of Claude Code in measurements 1.5–1.8: they were moved from repositories 9 together with the measurements, and `tools/tokens/prices.mjs` is checked against the prices of 1.2. A new quote for a measurement goes here.
