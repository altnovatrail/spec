# The mockup's sources

The files of the claude.ai canvas [Take from my paper](https://claude.ai/artifact/Vt9G8W392cMTTMykiByrcb#play-15dd8cd198dd), as they are, with the phone of RUN.md, T154, approved by the author on 2026-11-14:

- `Main.dc.html` — every screen, with its prop `device`: `ipad`, 1194 × 834 in landscape, or `phone`, 390 × 844 in portrait (the 2026-11-14 amendment to R148);
- `Phone.dc.html` — the phone's board: `Main.dc.html` with `device="phone"`;
- `Paper.dc.html` — the photographed page, synthetic;
- `canvas.json` — the boards and the canvas's notes.

The canvas itself holds version 28, without the phone: its account could not take the publish. Until a task publishes these files, they are newer than the canvas. The files of the canvas's runtime, `support.js` and `artifact-type/`, are not here: they are not NovaTrail's, and their license is unknown. The runtime of NovaTrail's own that opens these files in a browser, and the description of the screens, the style and the sizes, come with RUN.md, T156.
