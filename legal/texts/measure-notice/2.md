# Measurement: what happens to your samples

You are about to write samples for a measurement: they test how well AI reads handwriting, and nothing else. Before your first sample you have signed the form of this measurement with its organizer; this page says what that form says.

## Who we are

The samples page is run by NovaTrail's operator, ALT EdTech LLC, a limited liability company organized in Texas, in the United States: the controller of your samples and your form. The organizer acts for it, under its instructions. You can reach it by post: ALT EdTech LLC, 12828 Willow Centre Dr, Ste D, Houston, TX 77066-3043, USA; by telephone: +1 224 933 2890; by email: `altedtech.info@gmail.com`. Nor does it take one from a person who lives in Colorado or Washington, in the United States, whose laws on biometric and health data may reach the history of writing, until we publish a policy for such data.

## What a sample holds

- what you wrote, as strokes: their final shapes and also the history of your writing: each stroke with the kind of pointer that wrote it, the time and the course of the pen, and what you erased, cleared or undid, which stays in the history though the sheet no longer shows it; it holds no pressure. We treat that history as biometric data, and you give your explicit consent to it on the form;
- the crops of your answers, and their markup: what each answer says;
- your writer code, which the page makes and does not remember. It links your samples to each other; on them it stands in for your name, which only your form, kept by the operator, links to it. Keep it: you write it on your form, and you name it to withdraw.

The sample holds no name, and you write none on it — not even to erase it, since what you erase stays in the history: before a sample goes anywhere, the organizer looks for a name on it by hand, and a sample with a name is erased, and you write it again.

## Who sees it

- the organizer of the measurement;
- NovaTrail's program that measures the reading, and the AI provider whose models it measures: Anthropic.

Nobody compares writers by their handwriting, and no one uses your writing to tell who you are. Your samples are never published.

## Where it is kept

You save the file on your device and send it, with the scan of your form, to the organizer by the upload link they give you, which works for one day. If Safari saves your downloads to iCloud Drive, the file is in Apple's cloud too until you erase it; once the organizer has it, erase it from your device. The file and your form lie in the operator's closed storage, at Google Cloud in Virginia, United States; the organizer reads them there or on their own device and puts no copy anywhere else.

## How long

- 2 years from the session you wrote it in, and then it is erased, with the reports of NovaTrail's measuring program that name it. Your form stays 5 years after all the samples under it are erased, as the evidence of your consent.
- You can withdraw at any time by naming your writer code to the organizer, or by writing it to ALT EdTech LLC at the email or the address above; if you have lost the code, give your name and the date of your form instead: within 30 days the organizer erases your samples, their copies, and the reports of the measuring program that name them. What stays is what the AI provider keeps under its terms, and any copy on your own device or in your iCloud Drive, which you erase yourself. Conclusions written without your samples' IDs, such as a measured threshold, stay.
- Withdrawing does not make unlawful what was done before it.

## Why we may

We process your samples and your form with your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), and the history of your writing with your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)). We keep the form after the samples are erased to show that you consented (Articles 7(1) and 17(3)(e)). NovaTrail's privacy policy, in its sections "The samples page" and "Your rights", says the rest: your rights to see, correct and erase your data, and to complain to a data protection authority.
