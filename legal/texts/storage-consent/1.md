# Keeping works on our server

You are about to switch on keeping this learner's works on NovaTrail's server. Until you do, works such as drawings and essays are kept only on the device, where the browser may erase them on its own.

## What is kept

- each work of this profile, from now on, as the device holds it: its strokes and its picture;
- never the text read from it, and never the words of its feedback.

## What a work may hold

An essay, or any work in the learner's own words, may tell something personal — about their health, their family, or other people they name. Nobody reads the works we keep, we search them for nothing, and our server never sends them to an AI provider. By switching this on, you give your explicit consent to our keeping whatever the works tell, health among it.

## Who gives this consent

The person who gives consent for this learner gives this one too: you for your own profile; for a child's profile, the parent who gave consent for the child; and a learner who has reached the age of digital consent of their country, for themselves, in their own profile — the adult of the account cannot switch it on for them.

## How long, and how to stop

- The works are kept until you switch this off, erase a work, or delete the profile.
- Switching off is one step, as easy as switching on, where it was switched on: in the section for adults, or in the learner's own profile for a learner who gave this consent there. It erases the copies on our server at once, and the copies for recovery within a day; the works stay on the device and in the export.
- Withdrawing this consent does not make unlawful what was done before it.

The rest is in our privacy policy.
