# For you, the learner

This says what NovaTrail does with your work and your data, in short. The whole of it is in our privacy policy, which the adult of your account has read.

## Your work

- What you write stays on your device. To be checked, a picture or a crop of it goes to an AI provider, OpenAI or Anthropic, without your name or any ID, and our server does not keep it, unless keeping your works there is switched on.
- The picture shows whatever you wrote, so do not write your name or the real names of people you know.
- A program decides whether an answer is right. The tutor is an AI, not a person.

## What we keep

- a pseudonym, an avatar, your age category and country, and the settings of your profile — never your name, your date of birth, your school or where you are;
- what you have done and shown you can do, so that the next task suits you.

## Who sees it

- The adult who owns the account can see your progress: your attempts, how your writing was marked, and the mistakes you make again and again.
- Nobody else sees your progress, but the operator, when an adult reports a problem with the tutor's words at a reply. No one buys your data or advertises with it.

## Who decides

Until you reach the age of digital consent of your country, the adult of the account decides about your data. When you reach it, you are asked to confirm or withdraw that consent, and then you decide.

## If something is wrong

Tell an adult you trust, or call a helpline of your country: the app's "Help" names them. If you or someone else is in danger now, call your country's emergency number.
