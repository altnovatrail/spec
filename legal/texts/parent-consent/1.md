# Consent for a child

You are asking NovaTrail to let your child learn with it. Until you give this consent and the operator has checked it, your child has no profile and nothing of your child reaches our server but what the request for consent itself holds: the child's age in years, which our server reads once and does not store, and the age category and country, which it keeps. Read this, then sign the form at its end.

The whole of what we do with data is in our privacy policy, in its section "Children" among others. This text says what it means for your child.

## What your child does

Your child solves tasks from the modules you connect, writing and drawing by hand. A program checks the answers. An AI tutor reads the work, gives hints and explains, in words that suit your child's age. Your child always knows that the tutor is an AI and not a person.

## What we keep about your child

- a pseudonym you make up, and an avatar from a set — never a name or a photo;
- the age category and the country — never the date of birth or the age in years;
- the settings you choose for the profile: the interface language, reading aloud, the mode, the grade when a module asks for it, and whether you are asked before a solution is shown;
- what your child has done: what was read in each answer, the verdict, the hints given, how each attempt ended, the lessons, and what your child has shown they can do.

We do not ask a child's name, school, address, contacts, voice or photo, and we do not collect where the device is.

## Where your child's work goes

- What your child writes stays on the device. To be checked, a picture or a crop of it goes to an AI provider, OpenAI or Anthropic, through our server, which does not keep it. Before the work goes to a provider, the provider must have approved not keeping it, as our privacy policy says, with what such approval still leaves.
- No name, pseudonym or ID goes with the work. But the picture shows whatever is written on it, so the app reminds your child not to write their name or the real names of people they know.
- Your child's data goes to no one else: only to the service providers that run NovaTrail for us, for that and for nothing else, and never to train AI. We disclose it to no third party, but where the law requires it or a life is at risk, as our privacy policy says in "When the law requires it".

## What your child is told

- that the tutor is an AI;
- that you can see their progress;
- where to turn if something is wrong — to an adult they trust, and the helplines of their country, by name and number in the app's "Help".

## Your choices

- Keeping your child's works on our server is off until you switch it on; switching it on is a consent of its own.
- You can be asked before a solution is shown to your child; this is on until you switch it off.
- If the tutor's words seem wrong or harmful to you, report them at that reply. How we review a report is in our privacy policy.

## How your consent is checked

Print or copy the form below, fill it in by hand, sign it, and return a scan of it the way the app shows you. The operator checks it before your child's profile can be made. The scan is kept in the operator's closed storage, as long as the consent and 5 years after the profile or the account is deleted, and then erased.

## Withdrawing your consent

- You can withdraw it at any time, in the section for adults, in one step — as easily as you gave it.
- Withdrawing deletes your child's profile with all its data: on our server at once, in the copies for recovery within a day, and on other devices the next time they reach our server. Export the data first if you want to keep it.
- Of consent, only the record that it was given remains — its ID, its date, how it was checked and when the profile was deleted, with nothing about your child — for 5 years, and then it is erased by itself. Beyond that, what our privacy policy lists remains for its terms: our logs, Google's logs of sign-in and delivery, what an AI provider keeps of flagged content, and the data on a device that nobody opens again.
- Withdrawing does not make unlawful what was done before it.

## When your child grows up

When your child reaches the age of digital consent of their country, they are asked to confirm your consent or to withdraw it, and from then on they decide for themselves.

## Your rights as a parent

You can see your child's data, have it exported, stop its further collection and use, and have it deleted. If we change what we collect from your child or to whom we disclose it, we ask for your consent again before the change applies to your child.
