# Consent for a child

You are asking NovaTrail to let your child learn with it. Until you give this consent and the operator has checked it, your child has no profile and nothing of your child reaches our server but what the request for consent itself holds: the child's age in years, which our server reads and does not store, and the age category and country, which it keeps until the profile is made, you take the request back, or the account is deleted. Read this, then sign the form at its end.

The whole of what we do with data is in our privacy policy, in its section "Children" among others. This text says what it means for your child.

## Who we are

What the app can do — export, delete, withdraw, change a setting or your child's age — do in the app; for anything else, write from the email you sign in with, and name nothing of your child.

## What your child does

Your child solves tasks from the modules you connect, writing and drawing by hand. A program checks the answers. An AI tutor reads the work, gives hints and explains, in words that suit your child's age. Your child always knows that the tutor is an AI and not a person.

## What we keep about your child

- a pseudonym you make up, and an avatar from a set — never a name or a photo;
- the age category and the country, the date the age was given, and the age of digital consent of the country that the category was given by — never the date of birth or the age in years;
- the settings you choose for the profile: the interface language, reading aloud, the mode, the grade when a module asks for it, and whether you are asked before a solution is shown;
- what your child has done: what was read in each answer, the verdict, the hints given, how each attempt ended, how a piece of writing was marked against its criteria, the lessons and the modules, what your child has shown they can do, and why each next task was chosen.

We keep it on our server in the United States. We do not ask a child's name, school, address, contacts, voice or photo, and we do not collect where the device is.

## Where your child's work goes

- What your child writes stays on the device. To be checked, a picture or a crop of it, or the text read from an essay, goes to an AI provider, OpenAI or Anthropic, through our server, which does not keep it. Before the work goes to a provider, the provider must have approved not keeping it, as our privacy policy says, with what such approval still leaves.
- No name, pseudonym or ID goes with the work. But the picture shows whatever is written on it, so the app reminds your child not to write their name or the real names of people they know.
- Your child's data goes to no one else: only to the service providers that run NovaTrail for us, in the United States, for that and for nothing else, and never to train AI or to advertise. We disclose it to no third party, but where the law requires it or a life is at risk, as our privacy policy says in "When the law requires it".

## What your child is told

- that the tutor is an AI;
- that you can see their progress;
- where to turn if something is wrong — to an adult they trust, and the helplines of their country, by name and number in the app's "Help".

## Your choices

- Keeping your child's works on our server is off until you switch it on; switching it on is a consent of its own.
- You can be asked before a solution is shown to your child; this is on until you switch it off.
- If the tutor's words seem wrong or harmful to you, report them at that reply. A report holds the tutor's words, which may quote your child's work, and what you write, so write no name in it. The operator reviews it within 3 days, as our privacy policy says.

## How your consent is checked

Print or copy the form below, fill it in by hand, sign it, and upload a photo or a scan of it in the app, by the code of the request it shows you. The operator checks it before your child's profile can be made. The scan is kept in the operator's closed storage, in the United States, as long as the consent and 5 years after the profile or the account is deleted, and then erased.

## Why we may

We process your child's data with your consent (GDPR Articles 6(1)(a) and 8), verified as the United States' Children's Online Privacy Protection Act asks, to check your child's answers, to choose the next task, and to show you the progress. We keep the record of consent and the scan of the form after the profile is deleted to show that you consented (Articles 7(1) and 17(3)(e)). Our privacy policy, in "Your rights", says the rest, your right to complain among it.

## Withdrawing your consent

- You can withdraw it at any time, in the section for adults, in one step, after signing in again — as easily as you gave it.
- Withdrawing deletes your child's profile with all its data: on our server at once, in the copies for recovery within a day, and on other devices the next time they reach our server. Export the data first if you want to keep it.
- What remains: the record that consent was given — its ID, its date, how it was checked and when the profile was deleted, with nothing about your child — for 5 years, and then it is erased by itself; the scan of the form, for the same 5 years; a report of a problem you sent, for a year after it was reviewed, without the IDs of the account and the profile; and your emails to us, for a year after the email or our last reply to it, whichever is later. Beyond that, what our privacy policy lists remains for its terms: our logs, Google's logs of sign-in and delivery, what an AI provider keeps of flagged content, and the data on a device that nobody opens again.
- Withdrawing does not make unlawful what was done before it.

## When your child grows up

Once a year, when the country changes, and when the country raises its age of digital consent, the app asks you for your child's age and country again. When your child has reached the age of digital consent of their country, they are asked in their profile to confirm your consent or to withdraw it, and from then on they decide for themselves. When the age given again shows that your child is 18, the app tells them that the protections the law gives a minor no longer apply to them.

## Your rights as a parent

You can see your child's data, have it exported, have it corrected, stop its further collection and use, and have it deleted. If we change what we collect from your child or to whom we disclose it, we ask for your consent again before the change applies to your child.
