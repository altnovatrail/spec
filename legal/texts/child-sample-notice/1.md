# A child's sample: what happens to it

You are about to lead a session in which a child writes samples by hand, to test how well AI reads children's handwriting. Before the first session the child's parent or guardian has signed the parent's form with the organizer of the measurement, with the child's writer code on it; this page says what that form says.

## What a sample holds

- the final shapes of what the child wrote — no history of the writing, no pressure, no time — and the crops of the answers;
- the markup: what each answer says, which you write from the child's words after the child has written;
- the child's writer code, which links the child's samples, not the child's name.

No name, grade or age is in the file; the organizer keeps the band of the child's grades — 1–2, 3–4 or 5–6 — in their own record, by the writer code, as long as the samples. The child writes no name on a sample, and before a sample goes anywhere, the organizer looks for a name on it by hand; a sample with a name is erased.

## Who sees it

Only the organizer of the measurement. The samples are never published, and they go to no AI provider until the platform may give such a provider the work of a child under 13.

## Where the file goes

Save the file and send it to the organizer by the upload link they gave you for this session, which works for one day. If Safari saves your downloads to iCloud Drive, the file is in Apple's cloud too until you erase it: before saving, check that Safari saves downloads on the device. Once the organizer has the file, erase it from the device. The file lies in the operator's closed storage, at Google Cloud in Virginia, United States; the organizer reads it there or on their own device and puts no copy anywhere else.

## How long

- 2 years from the session, and then it is erased. A longer term needs a new form.
- The parent can withdraw by naming the child's writer code or the form: within 30 days the organizer erases the child's samples, their copies, and the reports that name them.
- The parent's form is kept 5 years after all the samples under it are erased, as the evidence of consent.
