# Your consent

You have reached the age at which the law of your country lets you decide for yourself what is done with your data. So NovaTrail asks you, not the adult of the account. If a parent consented for you before, this is where you confirm that consent or withdraw it: what we kept under it stays if you confirm, and from now on you decide. Read this, and decide.

## What you agree to

- We keep a pseudonym, an avatar, your age category and country, the settings of your profile, and what you have done: what was read in your answers, the verdicts, the hints, how your writing was marked, and what you have shown you can do. Never your name, your date of birth, your school or where you are. We keep it on our server in the United States.
- What you write stays on your device. To be checked, a picture or a crop of it, or the text read from an essay, goes to an AI provider, OpenAI or Anthropic, without your name or any ID, and our server does not keep it, unless you switch keeping your works on. The picture shows whatever you wrote, so do not write your name or the real names of people you know.
- A program decides whether an answer is right; the tutor is an AI, not a person.
- The adult who owns the account can see your progress: your attempts, how your writing was marked, and the mistakes you make again and again.
- Your data goes only to the service providers that run NovaTrail for us, in the United States, and to no one else — never to train AI, never for advertising — but where the law requires it or a life is at risk.

The whole of it is in our privacy policy, which you can read in the app.

## Why we may

We process your data with your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), to check your answers, to choose your next task and to show your progress. We keep the record of your consent after your profile is deleted to show that you consented (Articles 7(1) and 17(3)(e)). Our privacy policy, in "Your rights", says what else you can ask of us, and that you can complain.

## Keeping your works

Keeping your works, such as essays, on our server is off until you switch it on in your profile. That is your choice, and a consent of its own; the adult of the account cannot switch it on for you. If the adult had switched it on before you decide for yourself, the app asks you whether to keep it on: if you say yes, it stays on as your own consent; if you say no, it is switched off and the copies on our server are erased, once the app has offered you the export. You can say no; then the app does not ask you again for a year, and you can still switch it on yourself whenever you want.

## Withdrawing

- You can withdraw your consent at any time, in your profile, in one step — as easily as you gave it. Export your data first in your profile if you want to keep it.
- Withdrawing deletes your profile with all its data. The adult who owns the account can also delete your profile, with all its data.
- What remains: the record that consent was given, with nothing about you, for 5 years; if a parent consented for you before, the scan of their form, for the same 5 years; a report of a problem with the tutor's words, for a year after it was reviewed, without the ID of your profile; and what our privacy policy lists — our logs, Google's, and what an AI provider keeps of flagged content.
- Withdrawing does not make unlawful what was done before it.

## Later

Once a year, when your country changes, and when your country raises its age of digital consent, the adult gives your age and country again; if your country raises it above your age, a parent consents for you again. When the age given again shows that you are 18, the app tells you that the protections the law gives a minor no longer apply to you.

## If something is wrong

If the tutor says something wrong or hurtful, tell the adult of your account: they can report it at that reply. Tell an adult you trust, or call a helpline of your country: the app's "Help" names them. If you or someone else is in danger now, call your country's emergency number.
