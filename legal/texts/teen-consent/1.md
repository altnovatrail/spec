# Your consent

You have reached the age at which the law of your country lets you decide for yourself what is done with your data. So before you start, NovaTrail asks you, not the adult of the account. Read this, and decide.

## What you agree to

- We keep a pseudonym, an avatar, your age category and country, the settings of your profile, and what you have done: what was read in your answers, the verdicts, the hints, and what you have shown you can do. Never your name, your date of birth, your school or where you are.
- What you write stays on your device. To be checked, a picture or a crop of it goes to an AI provider, OpenAI or Anthropic, without your name or any ID, and our server does not keep it, unless you switch keeping your works on. The picture shows whatever you wrote, so do not write your name or the real names of people you know.
- A program decides whether an answer is right; the tutor is an AI, not a person.
- The adult who owns the account can see your progress.

The whole of it is in our privacy policy.

## Keeping your works

Keeping your works, such as essays, on our server is off until you switch it on in your profile. That is your choice, and a consent of its own; the adult of the account cannot switch it on for you.

## Withdrawing

- You can withdraw your consent at any time, in your profile, in one step — as easily as you gave it.
- Withdrawing deletes your profile with all its data. What remains is only the record that consent was given, with nothing about you, for 5 years.
- Withdrawing does not make unlawful what was done before it.

## If something is wrong

Tell an adult you trust, or call a helpline of your country: the app's "Help" names them. If you or someone else is in danger now, call your country's emergency number.
