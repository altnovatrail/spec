# Measurement: consent form

Sign this before your first sample of the measurement, and give it to its organizer. The page that you write on says the same in its notice.

## What you consent to

- You write handwriting samples for a measurement that tests how well AI reads handwriting.
- Each sample holds your strokes with the history of your writing — the time and the course of the pen, with no pressure — which we treat as biometric data; the crops of your answers and their markup; and a writer code that links your samples, not your name.
- The organizer of the measurement, the runner that measures, and the AI provider whose models are measured — Anthropic — receive your samples. Nobody compares writers by their handwriting or uses it to tell who you are, and the samples are never published.
- The samples are kept in the operator's closed storage, at Google Cloud in Virginia, United States, for 2 years from the session, and then erased. This form is kept there as the evidence of your consent for 5 years after all the samples under it are erased, and then erased too.
- You can withdraw at any time by naming your writer code to the organizer, who erases your samples, their copies and the reports of the runner that name them within 30 days. What stays is what the AI provider keeps under its terms, any copy on your own device, and conclusions written without your samples' IDs.
- You send the samples and the scan of this form by an upload link the organizer gives you, which works for one day.
- The page is opened from a file downloaded from NovaTrail's releases on GitHub, which sees the IP address of whoever downloads it, or from NovaTrail's site, where Firebase Hosting logs the IP address of the device for a few months.
- You write no name on a sample. A sample with a name is erased, and you write it again.

## Your declarations

- I am 18 or older, and under the laws of where I live I may make this agreement.
- I give my explicit consent to the processing of the history of my writing in my samples, for this measurement.
- Withdrawing does not make unlawful what was done before it.
