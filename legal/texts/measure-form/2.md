# Measurement: consent form

Sign this before your first sample of the measurement and show it to its organizer; then send its scan, with your samples, by the upload link they give you. The page that you write on says the same in its notice.

## What you consent to

- You write handwriting samples for a measurement that tests how well AI reads handwriting.
- Each sample holds your strokes with the history of your writing: each stroke with the kind of pointer that wrote it, the time and the course of the pen, and what you erased, cleared or undid, which stays in the history though the sheet no longer shows it; it holds no pressure — which we treat as biometric data; the crops of your answers and their markup; and a writer code that links your samples; on them it stands in for your name, which only this form links to it.
- ALT EdTech LLC, a limited liability company organized in Texas, which operates NovaTrail, is the controller of your samples and this form; the organizer acts for it. Its contacts are in the notice of the page and in NovaTrail's privacy policy: ALT EdTech LLC, 12828 Willow Centre Dr, Ste D, Houston, TX 77066-3043, USA; +1 224 933 2890; `altedtech.info@gmail.com`, a Gmail address that Google runs under its own terms.
- The organizer of the measurement, NovaTrail's program that measures, and the AI provider whose models are measured — Anthropic — receive your samples. Nobody compares writers by their handwriting or uses it to tell who you are, and the samples are never published.
- The samples are kept in the operator's closed storage, at Google Cloud in Virginia, United States, for 2 years from the session, and then erased, with the reports of the measuring program that name them. This form is kept there as the evidence of your consent for 5 years after all the samples under it are erased, and then erased too.
- You can withdraw at any time by naming your writer code to the organizer, or by writing it to ALT EdTech LLC, or, if you have lost the code, by your name and the date of this form: the organizer erases your samples, their copies and the reports of the measuring program that name them within 30 days. What stays is what the AI provider keeps under its terms, any copy on your own device or in your iCloud Drive, and conclusions written without your samples' IDs.
- You send the samples and the scan of this form by an upload link the organizer gives you, which works for one day. If Safari saves your downloads to iCloud Drive, the file is in Apple's cloud too until you erase it; once the organizer has it, erase it from your device. The organizer reads it in the operator's closed storage or on their own device and puts no copy anywhere else.
- The page is opened from a file downloaded from NovaTrail's releases on GitHub, which sees the IP address of whoever downloads it, or from NovaTrail's site, where Firebase Hosting logs the IP address of the device for a few months.
- Withdrawing does not make unlawful what was done before it.
- You write no name on a sample, not even to erase it. A sample with a name is erased, and you write it again.
- We process your samples and this form with your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), and the history of your writing with your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)), and keep this form after the samples are erased to show that you consented (Articles 7(1) and 17(3)(e)). NovaTrail's privacy policy says the rest, your rights among it.
