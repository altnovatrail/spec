# For you

This is about you and NovaTrail. A grown-up can read it with you.

## Your work

- You write and draw your answers.
- A computer program checks them.
- The tutor is an AI: a computer program, not a person. It helps you with hints.
- To read your work, the tutor gets a picture of it. It does not get your name.
- So do not write your name on your work, or the names of people you know.

## What we keep

- On NovaTrail you have a made-up name, not your real one.
- We keep what you did, so that we can give you the right next task.

## Who sees it

- The grown-up of your account can see how you are doing.
- Nobody else can see it.

## If something is wrong

- If something makes you sad or scared, tell a grown-up you trust.
- You can also call a helpline. The "Help" page shows its number.
- If you are in danger right now, tell a grown-up near you, or call the emergency number.
