# A child's sample: the parent's form

Sign this before your child's first session, with your child's writer code on it, and give it to the organizer of the measurement. The page that the session runs on says the same in its notice.

## What you consent to

- Your child writes handwriting samples in a session that an adult leads, to test how well AI reads children's handwriting and to test AI models again later.
- Each sample holds the final shapes of what your child wrote — no history of the writing — the crops of the answers, the markup that the adult writes from your child's words, and your child's writer code. It holds no name, grade or age.
- The organizer keeps the band of your child's grades — 1–2, 3–4 or 5–6 — in their own record, by the writer code, as long as the samples.
- Only the organizer of the measurement sees the samples. They are never published, and they go to no AI provider until the platform may give such a provider the work of a child under 13.
- The adult who leads the session sends the file to the organizer by an upload link and then erases it from the device; if Safari saves downloads to iCloud Drive, the file is in Apple's cloud until erased. The samples lie in the operator's closed storage, at Google Cloud in Virginia, United States.
- The samples are kept for 2 years from the session and then erased. You can withdraw at any time by naming the writer code or this form: within 30 days the organizer erases the samples, their copies, and the reports that name them.
- The page is opened from a file downloaded from NovaTrail's releases on GitHub, which sees the IP address of whoever downloads it, or from NovaTrail's site, where Firebase Hosting logs the IP address of the device for a few months.
- This form is kept as the evidence of your consent for 5 years after all the samples under it are erased, and then erased too.
- Withdrawing does not make unlawful what was done before it.
