# Privacy policy

This policy says what NovaTrail collects about the people who learn with it, why, where it is kept, who else receives it, how long it is kept, and what you can do about it. It covers learners of every age: an adult who learns, and a child or teenager who learns under an adult's account. The section "Children" is our notice to parents.

The English text is the one in force. A screen in another language shows its gist in that language beside it.

## Who we are

- by telephone: +1 224 933 2890;
- by email: `altedtech.info@gmail.com`.

The operator's email is Gmail, which Google runs under its own terms, not as our processor (see "Who receives data"). Please write no personal details of a child in an email: what the app can do — export, delete, withdraw a consent — do in the app.

We have not appointed a data protection officer.

The GDPR and the UK GDPR ask a controller outside the European Union and the United Kingdom to name a representative in each. We have not named them yet. Until we do, NovaTrail does not accept learners who live in the European Union, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, and a new version of this policy will name them.

A copy of NovaTrail that someone else runs on their own servers is theirs: its operator writes its own policy and its own terms, and we receive none of its data.

## The people in an account

- **The account owner** is an adult who signs in. They state that they are 18 or older, and they manage the account: the learners' profiles, the consent for a child below the age of digital consent of their country, the export and the deletion of data. A learner from that age on gives and withdraws their own consent, decides about keeping their works on our server, and exports their own data, in their own profile.
- **A learner** is the person who learns. Each learner has a profile in the account: the owner's own profile, and a profile for each child the owner is the parent or guardian of.
- **A child has no account** and never signs in. A child's profile is created by the account owner: below the age of digital consent of the child's country, only after a parent's consent has been confirmed; from that age on, at once, and the learner gives their own consent in it before their first work (see "Children").
- **The section for adults** of the app, where the profiles, the settings, the export and the deletion are, is closed by a PIN that the owner sets.

## The legal basis

Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR, each use of data has a legal basis:

- **An adult's own learning** is processed because it is the service the adult asked for under our terms of use (Article 6(1)(b)).
- **A learner under 18** is processed with consent (Article 6(1)(a)). Below the age of digital consent of the learner's country, a parent gives it (Article 8); from that age on, the learner gives it themselves, in their own profile. For a child under 13, the parent's consent is also verified, as the United States' Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) asks.
- **Keeping works on our server** is done with explicit consent, since an essay may tell something about health (Article 9(2)(a)).
- **The samples page** works with consent given on its forms: an adult volunteer's, explicit for the history of writing of a measurement sample, which we treat as biometric data (Articles 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a)), and a parent's for a child's samples (Articles 6(1)(a) and 8), verified as for a child's profile.
- **Messages to us and reports of a problem** rest on our legitimate interest in answering you, in keeping learners safe from harm, and in showing that we did (Article 6(1)(f)); answering a request about your rights is also our duty under these laws (Article 6(1)(c)).
- **Sharing what is needed to protect a life**, when a life is at risk, rests on the vital interests of the person (Articles 6(1)(d) and 9(2)(c)).
- **What remains of the consent of a learner under 18**, once it is withdrawn, or taken back once confirmed, or the profile or the account is deleted, is kept because we must be able to show that consent was given (Article 7(1)), and only as long as a claim about it could be brought (Article 17(3)(e)).

Withdrawing a consent does not make unlawful what was done before it. Without the account and a profile NovaTrail cannot work; the rest — the grade a module asks for, keeping works on our server, your own key — you give only if you choose to.

## Where AI comes in

- **A program decides whether an answer is right**, from the task's key, not AI. AI reads the work — handwriting, a drawing, an essay — and words the tutor's replies. A learner always knows that the tutor is an AI and not a person.
- **What goes to an AI provider:** the picture or crop of the work, or the text read from an essay; the task, and for an explanation its key and solution; for an essay, the criteria it is marked against; and, from the presentation style, only what changes the words, so that the tutor speaks to the learner's age.
- **What never goes:** a name, the pseudonym, an ID, or anything about the account owner. But a picture shows everything written on it. So before sending work that goes to AI whole, such as an essay, the app reminds the learner not to write their own name or the real names of people they know.
- **The platform's AI** is that of OpenAI and Anthropic, under their data processing agreements with us. Neither trains its models on the data we send. We use them only once the provider has approved zero data retention, or has excluded the content from its abuse monitoring: until then the platform's AI is closed. We do not use Google's AI models.
- **What zero data retention does not remove.** OpenAI scans images for child sexual abuse material, and keeps an image its classifier flags for review by a person; it may also keep, and have a person review, content its classifiers flag as breaking its usage policies. Anthropic keeps the results of its safety classifiers; it may keep the inputs and outputs of a flagged request for up to 2 years, and its classifiers' scores for up to 7 years.
- **A learner under 18** uses only the platform's AI, and only a provider whose terms allow its use for minors, with the measures those terms ask for.
- **Reporting a problem.** If the tutor's words seem wrong or harmful, the adult of the account can report them at that reply. The operator reviews a report within 3 days. If it shows that a child may come to harm, the operator acts on it: it reports to the CyberTipline what United States law requires (see "When the law requires it"), and where a life is at risk, it tells the emergency services of the child's country what they need to protect it. If anyone is in danger now, call your country's emergency number: do not wait for us.
- **Your own key** works only for the profile of an adult. A request with it goes to its provider under your account there: the provider sees your account and links each request to it, and the provider's terms are a contract between you and it. What goes in the request is the same as above.

## Reading aloud

NovaTrail reads the tutor's words aloud with the speech synthesis of the device, and it does not send the text anywhere for that. On Android and Linux the browser cannot tell the app whether the system speaks on the device or through a network service, so there reading aloud stays off until you switch it on, after the app has said why.

## Keeping works on our server

Works such as drawings and essays stay on the device unless keeping them on our server is switched on, for each learner's profile separately; it is off until then. You switch it on for your own profile and for a child's; a learner who has reached the age of digital consent of their country switches it on for themselves, in their own profile, and the adult of the account cannot do it for them. Our server keeps the work as the device holds it — its strokes and its picture — and never the text read from it or the words of its feedback. Nobody reads it, and our server never sends it to an AI provider. Switching it off, in one step, erases the copies on our server; the works stay on the device and in the export. The consent you give when you switch it on is a text of its own, which the app shows you then.

## The samples page

The samples page is a page of its own, apart from the app, where adult volunteers — and children, in a session that an adult leads — write samples by hand to test how well AI reads handwriting. It has its own notice, and each writer, or a child's parent, signs its own form before the first sample: they say what a sample holds and who sees it. The samples and the forms are kept in the operator's closed storage, never on our server or in a repository, for these terms:

| What | How long |
|---|---|
| A sample of a measurement, and a child's sample | 2 years from the session it was written in |
| The scan of a parent's form for a child's samples, and an adult's form for a measurement | 5 years after the samples under it are erased |
| A volunteer's form | as long as the samples under it are published, or 12 months after it was signed if none were added |
| A file that fails the organizer's check | erased at once, with all its copies |
| A sample that a volunteer gives to a module | published with the module under the Apache-2.0 license, for ever; this cannot be withdrawn |

A parent can withdraw a child's samples by naming the writer's code or the form: within 30 days the organizer erases them, their copies, and the reports that name them. A volunteer can withdraw the samples of a measurement too, by their writer code, and the organizer erases them within 30 days; the notice names what remains — the logs of the AI providers, for their terms, and the copy of the file on the volunteer's own device. A sample published in a module cannot be withdrawn.

A measurement's samples go to Anthropic, and an eval set's are run at Anthropic and OpenAI. A child's sample goes to an AI provider only when that provider may receive the work of a child under 13. The page is opened from a file downloaded from GitHub, which sees the IP address of the device that downloads it, or from Firebase Hosting, which keeps it a few months.

## Children

NovaTrail is for learners of any age, children among them. This section is our notice to parents, as COPPA asks.

- **A child never signs in.** The parent or guardian who owns the account creates the child's profile. Before that, they ask for consent for the child: the app asks the child's age and country, and keeps the age category, never the age.
- **The age again.** Once a year, when the learner's country changes, and when the country raises its age of digital consent, the app asks the account owner for the learner's age and country again. If the new age category needs another consent — the learner's own, or a parent's with a new code of the request — the profile waits for it before its next work.
- **How consent is checked.** Below the age of digital consent, the parent signs a consent form and uploads a photo or a scan of it in the app, by the code of the request the app shows; the operator checks it against that code before the child's profile can be made. The form asks for no name of the child: it carries the code of the request. Before NovaTrail opens to the public, we will review how consent is checked, and a new version of this policy will describe any way we add.
- **What we collect from a child**, and why, is in "What we collect and why": a pseudonym, an avatar, the age category and the country, the settings of the profile, and the record of learning. A child's work stays on the device, but for the picture or crop that goes to be checked, unless keeping works on our server is switched on for the child's profile (see "Keeping works on our server"). We do not ask a child for more information than an activity needs.
- **Who receives a child's data**: only the service providers in "Who receives data", for running NovaTrail and for nothing else. They are not allowed to use it for their own purposes or to train AI on it, and we disclose it to no one else, but as "When the law requires it" says. What an adult writes about a child to the operator's email reaches Gmail, under Google's own terms, so please write no personal details of a child there.
- **IP addresses.** Firebase Hosting and Google's sign-in service log the IP address of the device, to deliver the app and sign-in to it and to protect them from abuse, and for nothing else; they keep it as "How long we keep it" says. We keep the link of these logs to our own logs switched off, so the addresses never reach us, and they are used neither to contact anyone nor to build a profile of anyone.
- **Nothing a child does is public.** There are no public profiles and no messages between users, so a child cannot make personal information public on NovaTrail.
- **A teenager's consent.** From the age of digital consent of their country, a learner's profile is made at once, and the learner gives consent for themselves in it before their first work, and withdraws it there. A learner who grows into that age while a parent's consent stands is asked to confirm or withdraw it.
- **What the child is told.** That the tutor is an AI; that you can see their progress; and, under 18, where to turn if something is wrong — to an adult they trust, and the helplines of their country, shown by name and number in the app's "Help".
- **Your rights as a parent.** You can see your child's data, have it exported, stop its further collection and use by withdrawing consent, and have it deleted — in the section for adults, each in one step. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it, and it deletes the child's profile with all its data, as deleting the profile does; what remains after it is listed in "How long we keep it". Export the data first if you want to keep it.

## Your rights

- **In the app**, each in one step in the section for adults: see the learner's progress; change a profile's settings; export the learners' data in an open format; delete a profile or the whole account, after signing in again; withdraw a consent; switch off keeping works on our server. A learner who gave their own consent exports their own data, withdraws their consent — which deletes their profile with all its data — and switches keeping off, in their own profile. Your own key never goes in the export.
- **Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR** you may also ask for access to your data, its correction, its erasure, the restriction of its use, and its portability, and object to its use. Write to us at the address in "Who we are". We answer within one month, or tell you within that month why we need up to two more.
- **A complaint.** You may complain to a data protection authority, in particular that of the country where you live or work, or where you think the law was broken.
- **No decision with legal effect is made by automated means.** A program checks the answers and chooses the next task, and you can see why it chose it.

## What we never do

- no advertising, and no profiling for marketing;
- no selling of data;
- no public profiles, and no messages between users;
- no location of a device;
- no links out of the app;
- no reading of essays by us, but for the tutor's words an adult sends us in a report, which may quote an essay; and no search in them for anything;
- no inference of a learner's feelings.

## Changes to this policy

A change to this policy is a new version of it; an old version is never edited, and stays where we can read it. The app shows you a new version before it applies to you. If it changes what we collect from a child or to whom we disclose it, we ask for the parent's consent again before it applies to the child.
