# Terms of use

These terms are the agreement between you, the adult who owns a NovaTrail account, and the operator of NovaTrail, named in "Who we are" of our privacy policy. You accept them when you create the account. The English text is the one in force; a screen in another language shows its gist in that language beside it.

*Draft: the law that governs these terms, and where a dispute is heard, go here before this version comes into force (privacy 9, П-27).*

## The service

NovaTrail is a platform for learning: it gives a learner tasks and lessons from modules, checks the answers, and has an AI tutor give hints and explain. A program decides whether an answer is right, from the task's key. AI reads the work and words the tutor's replies, and it can be wrong: when it cannot read the work, the tutor asks the learner to write it again, and if its words seem wrong to you, you can report them.

For now NovaTrail is open only to those it invites, by a code, and it is free. The platform's AI works within a daily limit of spending: once it is reached, work that AI has to read is left unchecked until the next day, and the tutor uses its fixed texts.

We process the data of your own learning because it is the service you ask for under these terms. How, and what for, is in our privacy policy.

## Who may use it

- You must be 18 or older, and old enough under the law where you live to make this agreement. You state that you are 18 or older when you create the account.
- You may create a profile for yourself, and for each child you are the parent or guardian of. A child's profile is made only after consent has been given for the child as our privacy policy describes; a learner above the age of digital consent of their country gives consent themselves.
- A child never has an account of their own and never signs in.

## Your account

- You sign in with your Google account. Keep it safe: whoever signs in with it manages the account.
- The section for adults, with the profiles, the settings, the export and the deletion, is closed by a PIN you set. When the account also holds a profile of a learner under 18, your own profile is behind the same PIN.
- You can export the learners' data, or delete a profile or the whole account, at any time, each in one step.

## Your own key

- You may add a key of your own to an AI provider, for your own profile only; a learner under 18 never uses one.
- Requests with your key go to its provider under your account there, and the provider's terms are a contract between you and it. What those requests cost is yours to pay.
- If you choose to save the key on a device, it is not encrypted there: anyone who holds the device can get it, and the app says so before you save it. Our server holds it only while it answers a request, and never stores it.

## What you may not do

- create a profile for a child you are not the parent or guardian of, or give consent you are not entitled to give;
- try to get at another account's data, or to break or overload the service;
- use NovaTrail to harm anyone.

## Your works and our content

- What a learner writes and draws is theirs. We use it only to give the service.
- The official modules are published under the Apache-2.0 license, and their own files name what they hold.

## If something goes wrong

We do our best to keep NovaTrail working and its checks right, but we cannot promise that it will never fail or that the tutor's words will never be wrong. Nothing in these terms takes away a right that the law of your country gives you as a consumer.

## Changes and ending

- A change to these terms is a new version of them; an old version is never edited. The app shows you a new version, and you accept it before you go on.
- You may end the agreement at any time by deleting your account.
- An account that makes no request for 12 months is deleted, as our privacy policy says.
- If you break these terms, we may close the account; what remains of it then is what our privacy policy lists after a deletion.
