# Eval set: the volunteer's form

Sign this before your first sample for the eval set of a module, and give it to the organizer of the eval set. The page that you write on says the same in its notice.

## What you agree to

- You write handwriting samples for the eval set of a module. The organizer checks each by hand and adds it to the module's public repository, signing the commit with their own name under point (c) of the Developer Certificate of Origin below, which rests on your certifying its point (a) here. Nothing of you is in the commit.
- A sample holds the final shapes of what you wrote (not the history of your writing), the crops of your answers, and their markup — what each answer says, and for an essay its text and its marks. All of it is published.
- An essay is marked against the module's criteria by two people, each on their own; where they differ, a third decides, and one mark per criterion is published.
- Your samples are published with the module under the Apache-2.0 license, for ever. This cannot be withdrawn.
- In the module's tests, your samples run at the AI providers whose models are measured, Anthropic and OpenAI.
- You send the samples and the scan of this form by an upload link the organizer gives you, which works for one day.
- The page is opened from a file downloaded from NovaTrail's releases on GitHub, which sees the IP address of whoever downloads it, or from NovaTrail's site, where Firebase Hosting logs the IP address of the device for a few months.
- You write no name or signature on a sample, and only on made-up topics, with no names of real people. A sample that fails the organizer's check is erased at once, and you write it again.
- Your samples are used for evaluating AI, not for training a model; a use like that would need a new form.

## This form

This form and the organizer's record of it, which names the SHA-256 digests of the samples given under it, are kept in the operator's closed storage, at Google Cloud in Virginia, United States, apart from the samples: nothing of the form is in the repository. They are kept as long as any sample under the form is in a published version of the module, since they are the evidence of its license; a form under which no sample was added is erased 12 months after it was signed.
