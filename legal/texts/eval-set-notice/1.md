# Eval set: what happens to your samples

You are about to write samples for the eval set of a module: published samples by which every new AI model is tested before learners get it. Before your first sample you have signed the volunteer's form with the organizer of the eval set; this page says what that form says.

## What a sample holds

- the final shapes of what you wrote — no history of your writing, no pressure, no time — and the crops of your answers;
- the markup: what each answer says, and for an essay its text and its marks against the module's criteria — two people mark it, each on their own, and where they differ, a third decides;
- the license, Apache-2.0.

The sample holds no writer code, no name and no signature, and you write none on it. Write on made-up topics, with no names of real people. Before a sample goes anywhere, the organizer looks for a name on it by hand, and a sample with a name, or any that fails the check, is erased, and you write it again.

## Published for ever

Your samples are published with the module, in its public repository, under the Apache-2.0 license. Once published, they cannot be withdrawn: copies are out of anyone's reach. In the module's tests they run at the AI providers whose models are measured, Anthropic and OpenAI. They are used for evaluating AI, not for training a model.

Your form, and the organizer's record of which samples were given under it, are kept apart from the repository as long as any of your samples is published, as the evidence of their license; a form with no sample added is erased after 12 months.

## Where it goes until then

You save the file on your device and send it to the organizer by the upload link they give you, which works for one day. If Safari saves your downloads to iCloud Drive, the file is in Apple's cloud too until you erase it; once the organizer has it, erase it from your device. Until it is published, it lies in the operator's closed storage, at Google Cloud in Virginia, United States; the organizer reads it there or on their own device and puts no copy anywhere else.
